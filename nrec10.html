A Decade in the Cloud

It is a period of growing demand. Researchers, teachers and students across Norway need servers: fast, flexible and under their own control. Yet too many of them wait in line for hardware that never seems to arrive.

In November 2016, a small alliance of engineers from the Universities of Bergen and Oslo, with support from NeIC and Uninett, lit up a new community cloud. Built on OpenStack and named UH-IaaS, it entered production with two regions, BGO and OSL, standing guard at either end of the country.

Armed with open source and custom tooling forged in their own workshops, the engineers built, upgraded and rebuilt the platform, release after release, through migrations, maintenance windows and the occasional late-night alarm.

In time the cloud took a new name: NREC, the Norwegian Research and Education Cloud. GOLD images, object storage, DNS, shared HPC and GPU-accelerated instances joined its arsenal, and new institutions joined the journey.

Today, countless virtual machines power research, teaching and student projects, from a single test instance to entire scientific clusters, all just one Feide login away.

To every user who launched an instance, filed a ticket, reported a bug or forgot to delete a snapshot: THANK YOU.

To our partners and colleagues at every institution that trusted us along the way: THANK YOU.
